# Wave 4b engine contracts

- `Sim` starts at speed 1 in either phase. Speed stays in memory only; the outage cut preserves the current player speed. `game.setSpeed` cancels a temporary hurry and records the last nonzero player speed for Space to resume.
- `Member.freeAt` is an absolute game minute. Completing or releasing a manual task gives 45 minutes of autonomy grace; manual walking and Stay orders also receive grace. `waitingForAutonomy` excludes urgent hydration (<60), food (<25), or energy (<15). Active manual work always wins. `doing()` displays the remaining grace.
- Routine goals remain `autonomous: true`; routine completion never calls story or system completion. `autonomousLocation` persists after an autonomous arrival so proximity cannot complete a walk beat afterward. A new manual approach/move clears it.
- `map.routineSpots` caches personal/shared destinations. Routine objects and current/destination cells are reserved against other family members. Jess uses the bedroom aisle beside her bed because the bed has no directly walkable cardinal neighbour. Leisure routines rotate every 30–60 game minutes; only Marcus's dusk patio routine permits an outdoor destination. Theo's default follow mode has `routine: true` and selects adjacent common-room play spots; explicit child orders keep their existing follow/help behavior.
- `Prologue.skipAhead()` returns whether a quiet wait/task can hurry. It temporarily sets speed 12 and retains the exact prior speed, including pause. `guidance().canSkip` drives the Now-line button. `updateSkipping()` cancels when its particular wait/task ends or input/dialogue/manual work intervenes; main calls it before advancing the clock and the beat interpreter also calls it during updates. Choices never select themselves.
